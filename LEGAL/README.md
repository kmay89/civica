# LEGAL — Author Declarations (Non-Normative)

The two files in this directory are **author declarations** by Karl Meves, drafted in April 2025 and revised in February 2026.

Read them for what they are:

- They are **not statutes, contracts, or court filings.** They were not drafted by counsel, filed anywhere, or accepted by any authority.
- They **do not bind** readers, implementers, platforms, or models. Nothing in them creates an obligation on anyone but the author's own stated intent.
- They are **not part of the Civica spec.** The normative constraints live in [`CORE/`](../CORE/) and [`SPEC/`](../SPEC/). The citation a project may make ([`SPEC/citation.md`](../SPEC/citation.md)) does not cover this directory and must not mention it.
- **The SHA-256 in LEGAL-001 refers to a sealed file that is not published in this repository.** It cannot be verified from this repo. Do not cite it as proof of anything.
- **The non-harassment language is an ethical request by the author**, not an enforceable order. The author's privacy deserves the same ordinary respect anyone's does; this directory does not add legal force to that.

[`FRAMING.md`](../FRAMING.md) already states that Civica is not a legal instrument, not a regulatory proposal, and not binding on any person or institution. The directory name predates that framing and was kept rather than rewritten, because the archive rule applies here too: these declarations are preserved as written, with a one-line status pointer at the top of each.
